Privacy Policy

Effective August 15, 2026

TableTurn Digital is based in Franklin, Pennsylvania. This policy explains how information submitted through tableturndigital.net and TableTurn Digital's related lead-generation websites is handled.

Information we collect

When you contact us, we may collect your name, telephone number, business type, service interests, and message. Our lead-generation sites may also collect email addresses, ZIP codes, requested services, urgency, property details, and project descriptions. Basic technical information may be recorded by our website host and form provider for security and reliable operation.

How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, provide requested digital services, operate and improve our websites, prevent abuse, and connect homeowners with appropriate local contractors or crews.

How information is shared

Homeowner lead information may be shared with a contractor or crew for the requested matching purpose. Information may also be processed by providers that operate our websites and forms, including Netlify and Formspree. We do not sell personal information for unrelated advertising.

Cookies and analytics

Our sites use Google Analytics 4 to measure visits and successful inquiries. Google may set or read analytics cookies and process usage data under its own privacy terms. We use this information to understand site performance and improve our services.

Retention and security

Information is kept only as long as reasonably needed to handle inquiries, deliver services, maintain records, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may ask to review, correct, or delete information you submitted, subject to legal and operational retention requirements. Our sites are intended for adults and business operators and are not directed to children under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, call TableTurn Digital at (814) 209-8304.

Policy changes

We may update this policy as our services, providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date above shows the latest revision.
